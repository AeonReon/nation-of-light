Sound check

Every sound the room can use, one tap each. Tell Claude the names that sound good and the names that sound wrong, and the room is rebuilt from the good ones only.

QuietLoud

In the app these sit very low, under the voices. The slider starts near that level. On an iPhone use the side buttons; the slider only works on a computer.
