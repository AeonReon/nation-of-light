The room's beds (v85). Real recordings only: never synthesise ambience (the coded wind and fire pops were heard as "fuzz" and "a frog clicking").
v85 rebuilt every bed from the RAW source at 160k, with a gentle high-pass and LINEAR gain to -24 LUFS. The v81 beds were cut from storyreel's loudnorm-ed WAVs (loudnorm pumps) at 80k, and he said they "sound really bad, like something is broken". The brazier crackle and the trickling-water layer are OUT of the room: on a phone speaker a quiet crackle or hiss is exactly what a broken speaker sounds like.
Nobody can listen from a terminal, so beds are chosen by spectrogram (distinct calls over a dark floor) and then BY HIS EAR on /sounds.html, which plays every bed and every piece. Files named x-* are candidates waiting for his ear, not used by the app.
