The room's music (v85). His: he likes the original nocturne and did not take to the ten lesser-known Chopin excerpts of v83; he asked for the Moonlight Sonata and pieces with that weight. Every recording here is public domain or CC0, and every clip is cut from the start of the piece to a breath between phrases at about 75-95 seconds, faded out, 128k mp3.
LEVELS ARE LINEAR GAIN ONLY (towards -20 LUFS, never past the peak, never more than +10 dB). Never loudnorm a piano: its dynamic mode rides the level and the music pumps.
