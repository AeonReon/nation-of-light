# Writer E notes

## care.younger — Younger ones (18 rungs, from 10; older children, teenagers and grown-ups)
Order: eye level and one question, their game their way, a picture book, bringing a lone child into a game, waiting before helping, a fair head start (replaces "let them win without them knowing", which was a small deception), a kind no, comfort, a fair share between two, a game for three, a nervous thing done beside them, a mealtime or bedtime, an hour with the grown-up in another room, a half-hour walk, a one-hour activity for three or more, asking a parent what would help, eight weekly times, and last a week of daily help for a family.
Safeguarding: every rung is children in your own family or friends' children with their own grown-up nearby and in charge; the about says no rung has anybody alone with somebody else's child. The old "a whole afternoon on your own" and "a family new to the area through a season" are gone. Rung 13 (grown-up in another room of the house) is the most independent rung; under about twelve the grown-up stays in the same room.
Unsure: a grown-up with no young children in their family or friends has a thin way in (the notes name nieces, nephews and friends' children); I did not add volunteering with children because that brings vetting and rules that differ by country. `from: 10` may be high for the early rungs; the later ones need it.

## care.older — Older people (18 rungs, from 8; a child visits with a grown-up)
Order: a phone call first (no travel, no nerve needed), then questions (a day at your age, their work), a practical job, a half-hour visit, a photograph, their childhood place written down, a second contact that remembers last time, learning a skill from them, phone help with their hands on the buttons, something made or grown, four weekly contacts, an outing, a story written down and given back, talking about somebody who died (only if they want), bringing another person, inviting somebody who lives alone to a meal, and last twelve weeks of weekly contact.
Avoided: speak.listen's coaching and speak.people2's twenty-years-older conversation (this ladder is a relationship, not one talk); "shake their hand properly" dropped (varies by culture). speak.story rung 14 (an older person's story written down) is close to rung 14 here; mine gives the written copy back to them, but the overlap is worth a look.
Unsure: the bereavement rung is placed late on purpose, once there is trust; it is conditional ("if they want to talk about").

## speak.teach — Teaching (14 rungs, from 8; a child can teach a grown-up)
Order: write the steps of one small thing, test the steps on yourself, teach one person, hands behind your back, have them teach it back, fix the weak step and teach a second person, a week-later check, "I do not know, so let us find out", two people at once, a second way for somebody finding it hard, a bigger skill split into three lessons, those lessons to one person on three days, a group of four, and last the three lessons to a group until all can do it without you.
Kept the old rung 2 note (taking it off them is the common mistake) inside rung 4. Dropped "teach somebody who did not want to learn it" (not doable on demand, and a little unkind as a target). A grown-up teaching children does it with their grown-ups there.
Unsure: the many "teach somebody" final rungs on other ladders (chess, juggling, cube) are natural material for rungs 11–14; I kept examples generic so there is no clash.

## beauty.town — renamed "The beauty of where you live" (14 rungs, everybody, side by side)
Renamed from "Your own town": it sat next to belong.town1 "Your town, part one" and was really about seeing beauty in buildings and streets; the new name also fits a city or a village. Flag this for the owner.
Order: three things at your own door (room), look up above the shop fronts, the materials of one street, the best-kept building, a street of matching buildings against a mixed one, low sun early or late, where people stop, trees and water, the best view photographed or sketched, three building fronts drawn, one unpleasant spot and two small fixes, a thank-you to one owner, a planned half-hour walk past your ten favourite things, and last walking somebody round it.
Avoided: belong.town1 (new street, history, sitting to watch who passes, thanks to local groups), care.place (litter, reporting, the patch), beauty.look (pretty and ugly, windows, doors, photographing three good and three bad) and daily extras about when your street was built. The old "tell somebody who could do something about it" was dropped because care.place does the reporting.
Safety: stop and stand back from the road before looking up; a child with a grown-up at dusk and when knocking on a door.

## care.animals — Animals (19 rungs, everybody; no animal of your own needed; `place: animals` kept)
Order: watch one animal for five minutes, find out what it eats, meet a calm dog or cat with the owner's permission, five signs of relaxed or worried, water for wild animals for a week (free for anybody), feed somebody's animal while they watch, one cleaning job, walk a dog with the owner, a week in charge with the owner checking, a list of what well looks like, three questions to a vet or shelter or experienced owner, the yearly cost, contact a shelter, give the help they ask for, sit with a nervous animal, teach one thing with rewards only, two days in charge while the owner is away, show somebody new, and last twelve weeks of weekly care.
Free ways in named throughout: a neighbour's or friend's animal, birds and a water dish, a shelter.
Dropped: "raise something from young" and "deal properly with one that dies" (not doable without owning an animal, and death cannot be a rung you choose to do); "help something wild without making it tame" became the water dish.
Unsure / unverified: body-language signs are attributed ("commonly taught"); "people who are pregnant are usually advised to leave litter trays to somebody else" is attributed, not stated as fact; shelter volunteer age ("many ask for sixteen") varies by place. No `from`: the about and notes put a grown-up with any child around an animal they do not know.

## classic.beautiful — One beautiful thing a day (14 rungs, from 8; a family can do it together)
Order: one thing in the room, one outside, one in an everyday object, one sound, one made and one not made, start a notebook, seven days, draw one, copy a beautiful line, ten minutes with one famous painting or piece of music, show somebody a thing they walk past, thirty days, describe the painting or music from memory, and last sixty days read back with the three best chosen.
Avoided: calm.still1 rung 3 (five minutes looking at one beautiful thing) and calm.gratitude (a good thing that happened, the jar). The about says plainly this is not about what went well but about finding beauty. The old "commonplace book" became a plain notebook (the word is jargon for most readers); rung 9 is the commonplace idea without the word.
Unsure: sixty days rather than ninety for the far end, so the whole climb lands near three months; the famous examples (Vermeer, Hokusai, Bach, Beethoven) are named as famous things, not assumed local.
