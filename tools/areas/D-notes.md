# Writer D notes

## care.welcome — Welcoming (14 rungs, from 7, a child and a grown-up side by side)
Order: your own front door first (greet a visitor, coat and seat and a drink, get the room ready, see them out), then a group you already belong to: hello first, the three practical facts, stay with them, introduce them to one member, the unwritten rule, their name remembered next time. Then a new neighbour with a note, a one-page welcome sheet, asking to be the group's welcomer, and four weeks of doing it.
Avoided: people1/people2 names and general introductions (rung 8 is specifically a newcomer, and its note says so); speak.strangers counters; care.giving.
Unsure: rung 1 and the group rungs depend on a visitor or a new person turning up, so they can wait days (`with`, honest). Rung 3 (get the room ready) could come first as a `home` rung, but it only makes sense once a visitor is expected, so it follows the greeting. Kept `size: weeks` although four weeks at the end plus waiting for newcomers is closer to months.

## speak.humour — Being funny (18 rungs, from 8, side by side)
Order: one clean joke learned word for word, told with a pause, noticing what made people laugh, five jokes over five days, a joke that gets silence, a true funny story, a joke about your own mistake, saying a kind funny thing in the moment, what your ten favourite funny things share, writing one joke, testing and changing it, "Yes, and", a silent laugh, cheering somebody up (and stopping), three minutes of true funny stories, a trial on one person, five people, then five minutes to ten or more.
Removed: heckling, the comedy night, impressions (can mock people), "Tell one that dies" (figure of speech). The kindness rule is stated in `about` and rung 1: never makes fun of a real person, and every joke fine in front of a grandparent and a small child at once. The end is family parties / club or talent evenings, not stand-up venues.
Unsure: the example joke (the scarecrow "outstanding in his field") is a play on words that may not translate; the note offers a joke book or somebody's favourite instead.

## speak.story — Storytelling (19 rungs, from 6, side by side)
Order: today in order, a true story with beginning/middle/end, retell a known tale without the book, make one up from three objects and record it (the first rung doable alone), listen back and change one thing, the want-and-obstacle shape, the pause, voices, a bedtime story to a younger child, collect a family story with two questions, retell it, a round-the-table story, learn a folk tale from memory, a second telling made better on purpose, a planned laugh, a planned silence, five minutes to three people, teach a younger one the three parts, then ten or more people from memory.
Avoided: speak.stage (standing talks, rooms of thirty/fifty), care.older rung 12 (writing down an older person's story; here it is asking and retelling). Old "round a fire" and "in the dark" rungs removed (place-bound); a campfire is one example at the end.
Unsure: rung 4 assumes a phone to record on; the note allows telling it aloud alone. Folk-tale examples span cultures (Anansi, the Pied Piper, Momotaro, Stone Soup).

## food.host — Feeding people (18 rungs, from 8, a child hosts with a grown-up)
Order: the home table first (lay it, pour for others and sit last, notice what somebody needs, ask the household what they like and do not eat, one thing on the table nobody asked for), then one guest: invite and ask what they do not eat, paper plan, backwards timings, one dish the day before, host and sit down, write down what went well. Then three or four guests who do not all know each other, bringing in the quietest, letting a guest help, a same-day guest fed from the cupboard, six people for a normal day's food money (or everybody brings a dish), then plan and host eight or more with a reason, sending everybody home with something.
Avoided: food.cook (the cooking itself; the note in `about` points there), people2 rungs 10 and 13 (tea at yours, hosting two or three; rung 12's note says this time the meal is planned and cooked). Removed "have something go wrong and say nothing" (cannot be done on purpose; it is in rung 10's note now) and "come in under budget" (money-framed; rung 16 uses a normal day's food as the measure, with the bring-a-dish alternative).
Unsure: rung 1 describes a knife-and-fork table and says to lay it properly in your household's own way if you eat differently. Candle safety line is on rung 5.

## speak.hardthing — Saying the hard thing (17 rungs, from 10, grown-ups and anybody from about ten)
Order: write a two-sentence apology, say it with no "but", keep the promise for a week, a kind one-sentence no, a no with no invented excuse, "I was wrong", ask "what could I do better?" and answer only thank you, act on it for a week, honest feedback (one works, one to change), a small calm disagreement, disagreeing with somebody in charge, write three sentences for a hard thing, say it and listen, the longest-avoided conversation within a week, repair after a falling-out, speak up for somebody left out or blamed, and last calmly name what a group is avoiding and suggest a next step.
Avoided: nerve.asking (asking for things; the old "ask for something you have been putting off" is gone), nerve.no, speak.listen's disagreement rungs (listening to and saying back the other side), speak.stage's "I do not know". Removed "argue a case you disagree with" (debating, a different skill).
Unsure: safety. Rung 14 says do not have the conversation alone with somebody who could harm you; rung 16 says only where safe, otherwise get somebody in charge or your emergency number. A child's early rungs are with family.

## speak.connect — renamed from "Meeting people" to **Keeping in touch** (18 rungs, from 10)
What changed: the old ladder was a third copy of Meeting people (a stranger for two minutes, three introductions, a room where nobody knows you, follow up in two days, an introduction to somebody). It now has a different job: not losing the people you already have. Name, line, about and every rung are new; id, size and strand kept.
Order: a list of ten people, when you last spoke and one thing in their life, a message about their news, a call, sharing your own news, sending something that reminded you of them, birthdays into a calendar, a birthday message with one real thing, a reminder to ask how the big day went, a handwritten note, one regular time agreed and kept twice, a no-pressure message to somebody who has gone quiet, four weeks of two people a week, a hard date remembered, two more regular times, a group round-up twice, and twelve weeks with all ten heard from at least twice.
Avoided: people2 rung 1 (message three people not spoken to in a year), rung 14 (reconnect with an old friend in person), rung 20 (names for a bad day); care.older's weekly visits. No rung asks for a meeting in person except as one option for a regular time.
Unsure: whether "Keeping in touch" counts as a figure of speech under rule 5; it is the plainest everyday name for this, so I kept it. It sits in the Community area in school.json; it may fit Relationships better now. Rung 10 needs a stamp; the note gives a free alternative (a photo of the handwritten note).
