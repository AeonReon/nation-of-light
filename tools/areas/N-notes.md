# Writer N notes: six Creating order ladders (Home area)

## What I kept clear of
- `home.mend` owns repair technique (batteries, screws, glue, zips, sink trap, sticking doors, wall holes, replacing a part, fix three in a day, fixing and teaching for others). `order.broken` is about deciding (fix / shop / replace / go), safety, repair shops, disposal and looking after things, and points to the Mending ladder for how.
- `order.bedroom` owns under the bed, bedding, curtains, clothes piled on a chair and clothes laid out the night before, so the wardrobe and forgotten places do not repeat them.
- Daily items: no pen test (q-pen-test), no charger drawer (x-order-chargers: desk cables are only the ones on and behind the desk), no bottom row (q-bottom-row), no "prove the backup" rung (x-order-photo-check), no medicine date list (x-order-medicine-dates: giving only clears the box), fridge door done last inside a whole-fridge rung.
- Safety: nobody stands on a chair, step or ladder anywhere; lofts removed (need a ladder); high places are done from the floor with a broom and cloth. Cleaning products never mixed (bleach named). Out-of-date medicines to a pharmacy. Electrical things, batteries, bulbs, paint and chemicals to the place your local council lists. Gas and wiring for a qualified person. Selling hand-overs at the door or a busy public place, and no codes shared.

## order.desk: The desk (12 rungs, afternoon). Who: From about eight; a child and a grown-up side by side
Order: bare surface, the floor round it, the paper pile, one tray for what is waiting, drawers, cables, chair and screen height, light, one thing you like, clear at the end of one session, a week of end-of-day clears, two weeks and one line on what was easier. The surface comes first because it is the quick win; the papers need the surface clear; the tray catches what the paper pile left.
Extras: 1, 3, 5, 6, 7. Rung 3 changed from "every loose paper in the house" to "on and around the desk" (the house-wide pile is home.works1 rung 17); I kept it as an extra because it is the same job.
Unsure: "light from the side away from your writing hand" is given as what most people find, not a rule.

## order.wardrobe: The wardrobe (12 rungs, afternoon). Who: From about eight; a child with a grown-up
Order: one kind of clothing at a time, smallest first (socks and underwear, tops, then everything that hangs with a try-on, then shoes), bag what leaves, put back so every piece is seen, check what needs mending, pack away the other season, list what is missing, one load of washing straight back into the wardrobe, turn the hangers back to front, four weeks later decide about what is still back to front.
I replaced the old "everything onto the bed at once" start, because a whole wardrobe on the bed is not one doable rung and leaves the bed covered if the evening runs out. Selling is left to order.giving.
Extras: 8 (other season, now says what to do where weather is the same all year, and no loft), 9 (missing list).
Unsure: the "afternoon" size now ends four weeks out because of the hanger test; order.bedroom also ends two weeks out, so I followed that.

## order.broken: Broken things (15 rungs, weeks). Who: Grown-ups and teenagers; from ten with a grown-up
Order: list everything, make the dangerous ones safe straight away (it has to be rung 2, not later), give each a word (fix / shop / replace / go), quickest fix, one thing out the right way, find a repair place (guarantee first, repair café), take it, look for second-hand before new, find the manual by model number, the thing walked past for a year, let go of the "fix one day" thing, list to zero, one looking-after job, a written looking-after list, a weekly page for two months.
Dropped as repeats of home.mend: "fix three small things", "fix one that needs a part", "fix something for somebody else".
Extras: 1, 10.
Unsure: rung 8 involves a purchase; the free route (family, free-giving groups) comes first in the note.

## order.giving: Giving away (14 rungs, weeks). Who: From about eight; a family can do it together
Order: one bag, out the same day, then room by room from easiest to hardest (kitchen, books/games/toys, bathroom and medicines, towels and sheets, the avoided cupboard), then the harder letting go (a good thing you like, other people's bags, the expensive thing, the just-in-case thing, one sale), a month-on check of what you missed, eight weeks of a giving bag by the door.
The old "count what you missed" was the last rung; I moved it before the eight-week bag so the far end is the habit and the line matches.
Extras: 1, 3, 4, 5, 8.
Unsure: food banks, shelters and animal shelters taking toiletries or old towels vary by place; each note says to check or ask first.

## order.digital: The phone and the inbox (14 rungs, weeks, `from: 12`). Who: Grown-ups and anybody from twelve with a phone
Order: quick phone jobs first (unused apps, notifications, home screen, the two time-taking apps off page one), then screenshots, unsubscribe, inbox to empty once, backup, a year of photos a month at a time, forgotten subscription, signed-in devices, two-step sign-in, a week of empty inbox, ten-minute weekly tidy for two months.
Moved notifications and the home screen up (they are the fastest and make the rest calmer); moved "empty once" before "empty each day for a week". Added two-step sign-in, with the line never to give the code to anybody.
Extras: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 (all kept in similar form; rung 8's test changed "would be sick to lose" to "would be most sorry to lose").
Unsure: `from: 12` is my call; a younger child with a phone can do rungs 1 to 5 with a grown-up. The bank statement in rung 10 is for whoever pays; the note says to look through the list with them.

## order.forgotten: The forgotten places (14 rungs, weeks). Who: Grown-ups and teenagers; from ten with a grown-up
Order: light switches and handles (new, easy start), blinds and rail from the floor, one room's windows, under the sink and the bin, the fridge, tops and skirting from the floor, under and behind the furniture, the front door, descale or a filter, the oven, the things that clean other things, the place nobody has looked at in years, a written list of your own forgotten places, one a week for two months.
Dropped "wash the curtains" (order.bedroom rung 6). Removed "stand on a steady step" and "top of the wardrobe" and "behind the washing machine" (climbing or heavy). The oven uses bicarbonate paste, no shop oven cleaner, and the note says a grown-up does it.
Extras: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12.
Unsure: rung 1 is new, so it has `mins` but no `extra`. The kettle vinegar method is kept from the old note with "never add any other cleaner to vinegar".
