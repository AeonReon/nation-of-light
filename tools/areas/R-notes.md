# Writer R notes: Fun and creativity, performance ladders

Every trick method was worked through step by step before writing (see "Checked" lines). None of the old rungs had a `twist`, so none were kept. Ball juggling is left to `body.juggle`; storytelling, talks and jokes to the `speak.*` ladders.

## magic.coins: Coin tricks (20 rungs, from 8; who: children and grown-ups alike)
Order: a real hand-over at the mirror (the model every fake has to match), French drop, eyes on the empty hand, a vanish for one person, back from beside the ear, finger palm held for ten minutes, the pretend hand-over, then tricks built only from those: travel, small coin into big coin, angle check with a helper, coin through the table, borrowed coin, words, a three-trick routine run alone, then for one person, asked-again, keeping the secret, a planned drop, teaching, a three-minute routine for five or more.
Checked: French drop (coin retained in the left fingers); travel uses the pretend hand-over so the coin is already in the right fingers; the change trick has the big coin finger-palmed in the right hand during the French drop; the table trick has the left hand with the coin go under the table.
Unsure: the old "surrounded" and "four coins" rungs were dropped as not doable with these methods in three months. The ear rung says ask first and do not touch.

## magic.cards: Card tricks (20 rungs, from 8; who: children and grown-ups alike)
Order: self-working first (21-card trick alone, then with "abracadabra" spelling for one person), overhand shuffle, key card trick, saying what you do not what will happen, cross-cut force alone, then with a written prediction, two shuffles that bring a card back to the top, a chosen-card reveal with them, double lift, the card that changes on the table, the 21-card trick with them doing all the dealing, borrowed pack, a plan for when a trick goes wrong, order the act, five timed runs, act at home, angle check, teaching, the five-minute act for five or more.
Checked: 21 cards over three deals ends at position 11 whichever way each column is picked up; ABRACADABRA is 11 letters; complete cuts keep the key card right before the chosen card (edge case: key last, chosen first, said in the note); cross-cut force; overhand shuffle "run one card, then shuffle off" puts top to bottom and "shuffle off, drop the last card" puts bottom to top; double lift with the chosen card on top shows the card under it, and the single top card dealt is theirs.
Unsure: nothing on the methods. The prediction card has to be set on top before the act, said in rungs 15 and 20.

## magic.mentalism: renamed "Mind-reading tricks" (20 rungs, from 9; who: children and grown-ups alike)
Renamed so the name itself says trick. Order: always-five puzzle on paper, then for a person, 1089 on paper, 1089 as a prediction, the pause before a reveal, learn page 108 word 9, the book test (1089 as page 108, word 9), the dice stack on your own, then performed, the honest answer when asked (never claim a power), one-ahead on paper then for a person (its last question is 1089 so the first slip is known), black-object helper code practised then performed, centre-tear by touch, then the torn-paper animal with four or more, order a set with an opening sentence that says they are tricks, two timed runs, teaching, the ten-minute set for five or more.
Checked: always-five with 37; 1089 with 742 and 853, and the "digits differ by 2 or more" rule (a difference of 1 gives 99); dice stack hidden total = 21 minus the top face; the one-ahead order; in a 3x3 tear the centre piece is the only one with four torn edges.
Dropped: body-language guessing, "get one wrong on purpose" and "take longer than feels comfortable" (all edge towards claiming real power). What people choose is only numbers, colours, animals, foods and objects; no cold reading.
Unsure: the book test needs a book with at least 108 pages.

## magic.mime: Mime and clowning (14 rungs; was 12, size weeks; who: everybody side by side)
Order: a mimed cup at the mirror, five actions guessed, an object that stays where you left it, invisible wall, wall held still a minute (old note kept in new words), heavy and light, rope pull, walk on the spot, a three-part silent story, the clown double-take, a laugh from a pretend trouble with an object, a two-minute piece at the mirror, a mime game for younger children, the piece for five or more.
Kindness rule stated in the about and in rung 11: the trouble is always with a thing or yourself, never the watcher or any real person.
Unsure: the walk on the spot is described as step forward / slide back alternately, which keeps you in one place; it takes a lot of practice to look smooth, and the note says so. Socks on a smooth floor carry a note to go carefully.

## magic.puppet: Puppets (14 rungs; was 12, size weeks; who: everybody side by side)
Order: sock puppet, mouth on each word (thumb as the jaw), looking (old note kept), breathing, a voice and a name, asks a person questions, paper-bag puppet, a second voice, two puppets talking, a six-line story plan, a stage from a table and sheet or a box, two rehearsals, a show for two or more, the five-minute show for younger children and each child talks to a puppet.
Safety: a grown-up cuts and helps with needles; buttons only on puppets kept from under-threes. The no-paper-bag option is a second sock puppet.

## magic.juggleclub: Circus skills (20 rungs, from 7; who: from about seven, side by side)
Order by prop, easiest and softest first: sock poi (one hand, other hand and other direction, two together, two opposite, figure of eight), hoop (on the arm, roll-back, waist, arm to arm), plastic spinning plate (start, a minute passing hands, small toss), diabolo (start, a level minute, small toss), then a one-minute routine, a planned drop, two minutes to music, teaching sock poi, performing for five or more.
Removed football keepy-ups, club throwing, devil sticks and the "day out" rung. Only plastic spinning plates, never kitchen ones; diabolo tosses stay below head height.
Unsure: which way to steer a tipping diabolo. I did not state a direction; the notes say to turn your body or move a stick a little and find which way straightens it. Hoop, plate and diabolo may need borrowing; each note names schools, youth clubs and circus groups. I avoided calling a direction "forward" for poi because people use the word differently.
