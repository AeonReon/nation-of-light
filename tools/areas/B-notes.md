## mind.findout — Finding things out (14 rungs, from: 12)
- Order: write questions down → ask a person → search with key words → two websites → who wrote it and when → do a practical how-to → a well-built AI question → check an answer against a better source → trace a famous saying → read the original → ring the place that knows → write your belief before and after → a week on one bigger question → show somebody how you found it.
- Asking a person comes first because it needs no skill; searching and checking come before AI so the checking habit exists when AI arrives.
- Avoided mind.learn1's "look up one thing you wondered about" (here the first rung is only writing questions down), its no-screen answer and its bad-argument rung.
- Unsure: `from: 12` because of the AI and online rungs (most AI tools set a minimum age). The notes on rungs 6 and 11 say a younger teenager does them with a grown-up. The goldfish memory and knuckle cracking examples are topics to look up, not claims.

## home.mend — Mending (20 rungs)
- Order: batteries → the right screwdriver → write down what you must never do yourself (gas, mains wiring, roofs) → glue a thing and check it a week later → mend a book → fix a screw that spins in its hole → patch or darn → a zip → the U-bend → a sticking door or drawer → take a small thing apart → fill a hole → paint the patch → three repairs in a day → replace a part → watch somebody mend → put a mending box together → the thing somebody gave up on → fix for somebody else → teach somebody, hands off.
- The safety-limits rung is third on purpose, before any tool work. Safety lines are in the notes: button batteries, superglue, no drain-cleaner mixing, never open mains items, microwaves, TVs or chargers, a dust mask for sanding, and asking the landlord. It says a grown-up is needed for the sink, taking things apart, superglue and taking curtains down.
- Avoided home.works1's repairs: a loose handle, a squeaky hinge, a wobbly chair, a fuse, a trip switch, a radiator, a button or hem, and sharpening knives. I also dropped the old "change a plug" rung because it is mains wiring.
- **Please verify:** the Northern Ireland gas emergency number, 0800 002 001 (I am fairly sure, not certain), and that Gas Safe registration is the requirement in NI. "Supermarket battery box" is common UK practice.

- Everything is practised alone before anybody hears it, because the old ladder's rung 1 was singing where somebody can hear. That rung is also music.play1's rung 1, so it is gone.
- Unsure: I dropped the harmony rung (hard to describe clearly and doable within three months). The note-matching method (a "wobble" when slightly off) is a plain description, not a claim. "Many choirs welcome a newcomer" is softened and not checked.
